ABOUT ME

Luxury Travel Storyteller From India

I'm Neel Kothari, a luxury travel storyteller passionate about showcasing premium destinations through cinematic Reels, photography, and authentic experiences.

My journey has taken me from the skyscrapers of Dubai and the overwater villas of the Maldives to the culture of Japan, the beaches of Bali, the landscapes of Cape Town, and many incredible destinations across India.

I collaborate with luxury hotels, tourism boards, airlines, and travel brands to create engaging visual content that inspires people to explore the world.
